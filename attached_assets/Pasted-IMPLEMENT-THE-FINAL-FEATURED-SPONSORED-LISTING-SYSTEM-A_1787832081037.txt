IMPLEMENT THE FINAL FEATURED/SPONSORED LISTING SYSTEM AND CURRENT CAMPAIGN LEADER DISPLAY.

IMPORTANT:
Do not replace or break the existing campaign ranking, bidding, payment, invoice, demo, authentication, or admin functionality.

We need TWO DISTINCT systems:

1. PAID FEATURED/SPONSORED LISTINGS
2. CURRENT LIVE CAMPAIGN LEADER

They must remain completely separate in the data model and UI.

==================================================
1. FEATURED / SPONSORED LISTINGS
==================================================

Create/support exactly 4 Featured/Sponsored advertising positions.

These are PAID promotional placements.

Each position lasts for 7 days.

The four positions should be:

Featured Slot #1
Featured Slot #2
Featured Slot #3
Featured Slot #4

These are NOT the same thing as campaign ranking positions.

A listing can be:

- Sponsored only
- Campaign participant only
- Both sponsored and campaign participant
- Neither

Do not mix the two concepts.

==================================================
2. SPONSORED LISTING ORDER
==================================================

The four sponsored listings should be ordered by their active sponsorship placement.

If the business purchases Slot #1, it occupies Featured Slot #1.

If Slot #1 is unavailable, the next available paid slot can be purchased.

Each sponsorship must have:

- listing_id
- sponsor/owner
- slot_number
- start_at
- expires_at
- payment_id
- status
- created_at
- updated_at

Statuses should support at minimum:

active
expired
pending
cancelled

Do NOT hardcode sponsors.

All data must come from the database.

==================================================
3. 7-DAY EXPIRATION
==================================================

Every sponsored placement lasts exactly 7 days from its confirmed activation/start time.

When expires_at is reached:

- Remove it from active sponsored listings.
- Make that slot available again.
- Do not display it in the active sponsored section.
- Keep the historical sponsorship/payment record for admin reporting.
- Do not delete the listing itself.

Use server/database time rather than the visitor's local device clock.

15. SPONSORED POPUP RULES
==================================================

Popup rules:

- Show active sponsors only.
- Maximum 4 sponsors.
- Never show expired sponsors.
- Never show cancelled sponsors.
- Do not show if there are zero active sponsors.
- Do not show repeatedly on every page.
- Respect a frequency cap using local/session storage or existing visitor mechanism.
- Provide obvious close button.
- Do not interfere with navigation.
- Do not show the popup to the sponsor as a special exception unless existing UX rules require it.

Track:

popup_impression
popup_click

==================================================
19. FINAL BUSINESS MODEL
==================================================

The final system should effectively create TWO revenue/visibility products:

PRODUCT 1:
LIVE CAMPAIGN

Businesses pay to compete for ranking positions.

Ranking is determined by the existing campaign mechanics.

PRODUCT 2:
FEATURED SPONSORSHIP

Businesses pay for guaranteed promotional visibility for 7 days.

They receive:

- Featured/Sponsored listing near the bottom
- Entry popup exposure
- Sponsor badge
- Click/impression tracking
- Analytics
- Invoice

These two products must remain independent.

==================================================
20. FINAL ACCEPTANCE TEST
==================================================

After implementation test all of the following:

1. No sponsors:
   Discover page works normally.

2. One sponsor:
   One sponsor appears in Featured section.
   One sponsor appears in popup.

3. Four sponsors:
   All four appear.
   Popup shows all four.

4. Fifth sponsor:
   Cannot occupy a fifth active slot.
   Must wait until a slot becomes available.

5. Sponsorship expires:
   It disappears from active Featured section.
   It disappears from popup.
   Slot becomes available.

6. Campaign leader changes:
   Current leader updates independently of sponsorship.

7. Sponsor is also campaign #1:
   Both badges/statuses appear correctly.

8. Campaign ends:
   Winners are displayed according to existing winner logic.

9. Payment succeeds:
   Sponsorship activates correctly.
   Invoice is generated.
   Invoice is sent to customer and admin.

10. Mobile:
    No horizontal page overflow.
    Popup is usable.
    Bottom navigation remains usable.

11. Admin:
    Admin can view and manage sponsored placements.

12. Security:
    Only authorized admin users can manage sponsorships/pricing.
    Never trust client-side sponsorship status or expiration.
    Validate sponsorship/payment state server-side.

Do not modify unrelated functionality.
Run existing tests and add appropriate tests for sponsorship creation, expiration, slot limits, popup eligibility, analytics, and admin authorization.
