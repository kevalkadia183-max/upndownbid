========================================================
11. OWNER BID VS COMMUNITY SUPPORT
========================================================

These are completely different actions.

OWNER BID:

Money paid by the product owner to enter or increase the product's owner bid.

COMMUNITY SUPPORT:

Money paid by another user to support an existing product.

PENALTY:

Money paid by another user to apply a negative signal against an existing product.

Never mix these values.
