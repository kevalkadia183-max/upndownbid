IMPORTANT PRODUCT CHANGE:

REMOVE AUTHENTICATION COMPLETELY.

We do NOT want user accounts, login, signup, passwords, or a profile system for the public product.

The platform must work as a frictionless public marketplace/leaderboard.

Do NOT ask users to sign in before:

* Adding a product
* Supporting a product
* Applying a penalty
* Writing a review
* Viewing products
* Viewing rankings

The reference experience should feel like a simple public marketplace where someone can enter a URL, choose an amount, and complete a one-time checkout.

5. EMAIL INSTEAD OF ACCOUNTS

During checkout, collect an email address.

This email is NOT an account.

It is used for:

* Payment receipt
* Transaction confirmation
* Product management access if the person created a listing
* Review confirmation if appropriate
* Support/penalty receipt
* Fraud/security communication

Do not automatically create a user account.

⸻

6. PRODUCT OWNERSHIP WITHOUT ACCOUNTS

Because there is no login system, ownership must be handled differently.

When someone creates a product:

Store internally:

listing_id
owner_email
owner_name if provided
creation_transaction_id

After successful payment, email the owner a secure:

Manage Your Product

link.

Example:

https://example.com/manage/secure-token

The token must be:

* Cryptographically random
* Unpredictable
* Expiring or rotatable
* Stored securely
* Revocable

Do NOT put sensitive information directly into the URL.

This secure link allows the person who created the listing to:

* Edit product description
* Change logo
* Change screenshots
* Update website
* Increase owner bid
* View listing analytics
* View activity
* Manage product information

They still do NOT need an account.

16. ADMIN IS DIFFERENT

The public platform does NOT need accounts.

However, the platform administrator still needs a protected admin area.

Keep a separate admin authentication system.

Admin can manage:

Listings
Transactions
Reviews
Reports
Payments
Moderation
Fraud
Settings
Audit Logs

Do NOT expose admin functionality publicly.

⸻

17. ABUSE PROTECTION WITHOUT ACCOUNTS

Because there are no accounts, strengthen transaction-level abuse protection.

Implement:

* Rate limiting
* IP/request throttling where appropriate
* Payment-provider fraud signals
* Email verification where appropriate
* Duplicate transaction protection
* Idempotency keys
* URL duplication checks
* Review spam detection
* Review reporting
* Suspicious transaction detection
* Listing moderation
* Admin suspension

Do not rely on login as an anti-abuse mechanism.

Do not use invasive fingerprinting.

20. IMPORTANT

The absence of login is intentional.

Do NOT reintroduce authentication simply because ownership or reviews need an identity.

Use:

EMAIL + VERIFIED PAYMENT + SECURE MANAGEMENT TOKEN

instead of:

USERNAME + PASSWORD + ACCOUNT.

The goal is a frictionless experience:

Find → Bid/Support/Penalize → Checkout → Done.

This should be the primary interaction model of the platform.
