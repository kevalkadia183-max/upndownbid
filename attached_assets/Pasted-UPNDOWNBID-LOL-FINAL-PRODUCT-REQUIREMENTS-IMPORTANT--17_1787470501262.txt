UPNDOWNBID.LOL — FINAL PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS
============================================

IMPORTANT:
Do not treat this as a normal permanent leaderboard.

UpnDownBid.lol is a WEEKLY PUBLIC BIDDING COMPETITION for websites/apps.

Every campaign lasts exactly 7 days.
At the end of every campaign:
- bidding closes
- a winner is determined
- the winner is permanently saved to history
- that winner becomes the "LAST WEEK'S WINNER" shown at the top
- a completely fresh campaign starts
- everyone starts again from the minimum bid

Use the existing implementation as the starting point, but make the necessary frontend, backend, database and business-logic changes.

Use Outbid.lol / Polar-style public bidding and checkout flows as UX inspiration only. Do NOT copy proprietary code, branding, assets or exact designs.

==================================================
2. CORE WEEKLY CAMPAIGN MODEL
==================================================

Every campaign lasts exactly 7 days.

Example:

Campaign #12
Sunday → next Sunday

When Campaign #12 ends:

1. Freeze Campaign #12.
2. Stop accepting transactions for Campaign #12.
3. Calculate final rankings.
4. Determine winner.
5. Save winner permanently.
6. Mark Campaign #12 COMPLETED.
7. Create Campaign #13.
8. Campaign #13 starts fresh with the minimum bid.
9. Campaign #13 becomes LIVE.

The campaign lifecycle MUST be controlled by the backend.

Do NOT rely on browser timers or frontend JavaScript alone.

Use a reliable server-side scheduled process/job and database locking/idempotency.

==================================================
19. CAMPAIGN CLOSE
==================================================

At exact end time:

1. Lock campaign.
2. Stop new transactions.
3. Finalize verified successful transactions.
4. Calculate all final effective bids.
5. Calculate final ranking.
6. Determine winner.
7. Save winner.
8. Mark campaign COMPLETED.
9. Create next campaign.
10. Start new campaign.
11. Make new campaign visible immediately.

Only VERIFIED/SUCCEEDED payments count.

Pending payments do NOT count.

Use database transactions/locking/idempotency to prevent double finalization.

39. MOST IMPORTANT RULE
==================================================

The previous week's winner is NOT part of the new week's ranking unless someone explicitly makes a NEW transaction for the new campaign.

The previous winner is displayed at the top ONLY as:

🏆 LAST WEEK'S WINNER

The current campaign has its own completely independent ranking.

At the end of the current campaign, its winner replaces the previous winner in the champion section.

All previous winners remain permanently available in Winner History.

Implement this across the database, backend, ranking engine, campaign scheduler, frontend, product pages, checkout, activity feed and admin panel.

Do not make this a cosmetic UI change.

The weekly campaign lifecycle must actually work end-to-end.
