REDESIGN THE ENTIRE SIGNALRANK UI.

I reviewed the current implementation on mobile and the current visual design feels outdated. The functionality is moving in the right direction, but the layout, typography, cards, borders, spacing, and overall visual language need a complete modern redesign.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT simply tweak the existing CSS.

Treat this as a complete UI/UX redesign while preserving the existing functionality and backend logic.

The current screenshots show an overly heavy neo-brutalist style:

* Very thick dark borders
* Large rectangular boxes everywhere
* Heavy offset shadows
* Excessive monospace typography
* Oversized hero text
* Too much empty vertical space
* Cards feel like old design-system components
* Buttons are too large and visually heavy
* Financial information is not presented in the most intuitive hierarchy
* Mobile screens require too much scrolling
* The interface feels more like a design experiment than a modern product

Replace this visual language completely.

PRODUCT DIRECTION

SignalRank should feel like a combination of:

* Modern fintech dashboard
* Product discovery platform
* Social ranking platform
* Modern startup directory
* Financial activity feed

The product should feel:

* Premium
* Modern
* Fast
* Trustworthy
* Interactive
* Slightly competitive
* Data-driven
* Mobile-first

Do NOT make it look like a banking app.
Do NOT make it look like a generic SaaS dashboard.
Do NOT make it look like an old-school brutalist website.

Use a modern 2026 web-app aesthetic.
