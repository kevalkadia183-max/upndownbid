UPDOWNBID — LEGAL, SAFETY, CONTENT POLICY & USER PROTECTION SYSTEM

Implement a complete, professional policy and safety system for Updownbid.

IMPORTANT:
- Inspect the existing application architecture before making changes.
- Do not break existing authentication, Google login, PayPal, ranking, boost, push-down, campaign, listing, or payment functionality.
- Do not make unrelated UI changes.
- Reuse the existing routing, database, authentication, and design system.
- All policy text must be stored in a maintainable way so it can be updated later.
- The final legal wording must be clearly presented as Updownbid's policies and must not falsely claim certification or legal compliance.
- Do not claim that these policies override applicable law.
- Where applicable law gives a user a mandatory right, the policy must not attempt to remove that right.

2. TERMS & CONDITIONS
==================================================

Page title:

Terms & Conditions

Subtitle:

Last updated: [dynamic/current policy date]

Include the following sections:

1. About Updownbid

Updownbid is a platform that allows users to submit websites/products and participate in time-limited ranking campaigns.

Users may interact with listings through actions such as:
- claiming a ranking position
- boosting a listing
- pushing down a listing
- other platform features introduced in the future

Updownbid does not guarantee that any listing will receive a particular ranking, traffic level, sales, leads, search-engine position, visibility, or business result.

Ranking positions can change based on activity and the rules of the applicable campaign.

2. Eligibility

Users must provide accurate information when creating an account.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their account.

Users must not create accounts for fraudulent, abusive, or deceptive purposes.

If age restrictions apply under applicable law or payment-provider requirements, users must comply with them.

3. User Accounts

Users are responsible for:
- account credentials
- activity performed through their account
- keeping account information accurate
- notifying Updownbid of suspected unauthorized access

Updownbid may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these Terms, applicable law, payment-provider requirements, or platform policies.

4. Website Listings

Users must only submit websites they are authorized to promote.

A user must not submit:
- someone else's website without authorization
- malicious websites
- phishing websites
- malware
- fraudulent websites
- scam websites
- impersonation websites
- websites containing illegal content
- prohibited adult/sexually explicit content
- gambling or betting websites
- illegal lotteries
- deceptive financial schemes
- illegal investment schemes
- spam websites
- websites primarily designed for abusive traffic generation
- websites that facilitate criminal activity
- websites violating intellectual-property rights
- websites violating applicable laws

5. Ranking System

Updownbid provides a ranking mechanism based on the rules of each campaign.

Ranking is not a guarantee of:
- search-engine ranking
- website popularity
- revenue
- traffic
- conversion
- customer acquisition
- business success

Campaigns may have different rules, durations, minimum amounts, and ranking mechanics.

6. Payments

Payments may be processed through supported third-party payment providers such as PayPal.

Updownbid does not store users' complete payment-card credentials when payment is processed by the payment provider.

Payment-provider terms may also apply.

The amount shown before payment should clearly identify applicable charges and taxes where required.

7. Prohibited Activities

Users must not use Updownbid to:
- manipulate rankings through fraud
- create fake accounts
- create automated fake activity
- spam users
- abuse payment systems
- perform chargeback fraud
- exploit technical vulnerabilities
- attack the platform
- interfere with other users
- submit prohibited websites
- circumvent campaign limits
- manipulate click analytics
- manipulate boost/push-down activity
- use bots to create artificial engagement
- misrepresent a product or service

8. Platform Moderation

Updownbid may:
- review listings
- suspend listings
- remove listings
- freeze campaign activity
- suspend accounts
- restrict platform functionality
- investigate suspicious activity
- cooperate with payment providers and lawful authorities where appropriate

9. External Websites

Updownbid may display links to websites submitted by users.

Updownbid does not automatically endorse or guarantee third-party websites.

Users visit external websites at their own risk.

Updownbid is not responsible for third-party website content, availability, products, services, privacy practices, or transactions except where applicable law provides otherwise.

10. Intellectual Property

Users must have the rights necessary to submit names, logos, images, descriptions, URLs, and other content.

Users must not upload or submit material that infringes another person's intellectual-property rights.

11. Platform Changes

Updownbid may modify, improve, suspend, or discontinue features.

Campaign rules may change for future campaigns.

Existing paid transactions should be handled according to the applicable transaction terms and applicable law.

12. Disclaimer

Updownbid does not guarantee uninterrupted availability or any specific business outcome.

Nothing in these Terms excludes liability or consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

13. Governing Law

Use a placeholder for the final legal jurisdiction rather than inventing one.

Example:

"These Terms are intended to be governed by the laws applicable to Updownbid's operating entity and applicable mandatory consumer-protection laws. The final governing-law and jurisdiction provisions should be confirmed by legal counsel."

14. Contact

Provide the official Updownbid support/contact details from the existing application configuration.

Do NOT invent an email address.

3. WEBSITE & CONTENT POLICY
==================================================

Page title:

Website & Content Policy

Purpose:

Protect the Updownbid community and prevent the platform from being used to promote harmful, illegal, deceptive, abusive, or prohibited websites.

Clearly state:

Updownbid may remove a listing if the submitted website or associated content violates this policy.

PROHIBITED CONTENT:

A. Spam

- spam websites
- automated spam
- mass unsolicited promotion
- fake landing pages
- traffic-generation abuse
- repetitive submissions intended to manipulate rankings

B. Fraud and scams

- phishing
- fake login pages
- impersonation
- fraudulent stores
- fake investment schemes
- fake financial services
- deceptive offers
- pyramid/Ponzi-style schemes
- fraudulent fundraising
- identity theft

C. Malware and security threats

- malware
- ransomware
- credential theft
- malicious downloads
- exploit delivery
- botnets
- malicious scripts
- websites designed to compromise visitors

D. Adult/sexually explicit content

Do not allow websites primarily containing:
- pornography
- sexually explicit material
- sexual services
- sexual exploitation
- non-consensual sexual content

Any content involving sexual exploitation of minors must be prohibited and handled according to applicable law.

E. Gambling and betting

Do not allow:
- illegal gambling
- betting services where prohibited
- casino promotion where prohibited
- unauthorized lotteries
- gambling-related fraud

Do not assume that every gaming website is gambling.

If a legitimate skill-based or regulated service is submitted, it may require manual review depending on applicable law and platform policy.

F. Illegal activity

- illegal drugs
- trafficking
- weapons prohibited by law
- criminal services
- stolen goods
- hacking-for-hire
- illegal surveillance
- other unlawful activity

G. Hate, harassment and extremist content

Do not allow content that promotes:
- violent extremism
- terrorism
- hate-based violence
- targeted harassment
- threats

H. Copyright/IP infringement

Users must not knowingly submit websites that infringe third-party rights.

I. Misleading claims

Do not submit websites using:
- fake testimonials
- fake reviews
- deceptive pricing
- false claims
- impersonation
- misleading business identity

J. Dangerous or harmful content

Updownbid may remove content that creates a serious risk of harm to users.

Refund eligibility will be determined according to the applicable Refund & Cancellation Policy, the transaction terms, applicable law, and any mandatory consumer rights."

"Policy violations do not create an automatic entitlement to a refund."

But also:

"Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any refund, cancellation, consumer, or other legal right that cannot lawfully be excluded."

"Where a user violates the Website & Content Policy, Updownbid may remove the listing and restrict the associated account or campaign activity. A refund is not automatically guaranteed solely because a listing was removed for a policy violation. Any refund request will be evaluated under the applicable refund rules and applicable law."

For duplicate charges:

"If the same transaction is accidentally charged more than once, the duplicate transaction should be investigated and handled appropriately."

For technical payment failure:

"If a payment is unsuccessful, Updownbid must not treat the transaction as successfully completed merely because the user reached a payment page."

For unauthorized transactions:

"Users should contact Updownbid support and the relevant payment provider promptly."

Do not create fake refund timeframes unless the actual business policy has been decided.

16. AUTOMATED SAFETY CHECKS
==================================================

When a website is submitted:

Perform basic safe validation.

Check:
- valid URL
- http/https only
- hostname
- malformed URLs
- obvious malicious patterns
- duplicate submissions

Do NOT build an unsafe crawler that blindly executes arbitrary website JavaScript.

Do not fetch arbitrary websites from the server without appropriate SSRF protections.

If server-side URL fetching is already implemented, protect it against:
- localhost
- 127.0.0.1
- private IP ranges
- cloud metadata endpoints
- internal network addresses
- unsupported protocols
- DNS rebinding
- redirects to internal addresses

Use an appropriate safe URL validation architecture.

Automated classification should be treated as a signal, not an unquestionable legal determination.

High-risk cases should be flagged for manual review.
